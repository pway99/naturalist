# Vertebrates

Will catalog the vertebrates of Oak Vista — birds, reptiles, amphibians, and
mammals — alongside the other organism domains.

A planned organism domain. It follows the shared organism-domain blueprint that
the insects domain established, and is currently skeletal — module scaffolding
only (`vertebrates-api`, `vertebrates-core`, `vertebrates-repository-test` with
their poms), with no entities, queries, or seed catalog yet.
