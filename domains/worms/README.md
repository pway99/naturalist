# Worms

Will catalog the annelid worms of Oak Vista — chiefly the earthworms that act
as ecological engineers of the soil, tracking burrowing depth and casting rate
as indicators of soil biological health.

A planned organism domain. It follows the shared organism-domain blueprint that
the insects domain established, and is currently skeletal: `worms-api` holds an
early `WormSpecies` sketch with a small seed catalog, but the query, command,
and repository layers the blueprint calls for are not built yet.
